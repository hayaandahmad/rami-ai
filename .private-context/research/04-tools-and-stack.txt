AZURE OPENAI
------------
- Preferred enterprise AI API option for quality + privacy story
- Usually pay-per-token (pilot often cheap: cents–dollars per draft)
- Full cost details: azure-ai-api-costs.txt in this folder


DOCUPILOT
---------
What it is good at:
  - Template + data → generated Word/PDF documents
  - Low-code automation, integrations, delivery

What it does NOT fully cover for Rami:
  - Deep agent-led interviewing
  - Jordan/MODEE standards reasoning
  - Full RFP narrative drafting from incomplete answers

Verdict:
  Useful as optional formatter/delivery layer.
  Not sufficient alone as Rami.

Note: Document scoring is out of scope for Phase 1.
